# CP2 Interview findings

We interviewed 21 students (all students; drivers said they are not allowed to give interviews). This is our summary of what they told us, not exact quotes.

## What came up again and again
1. **Carts wait until they're full before they leave.** Even if a cart is at the stop you can't just get on and go.
2. **There are far more students than carts.** Carts fill fast; whoever doesn't fit waits for the next one.
3. **Nobody knows where the carts are or when the next one is coming.** Students walk to the stop and hope.
4. **New hardware on the carts would be hard.** The carts already run, so a fix shouldn't need devices fitted to them.

## An idea students gave us
An app that shows where the carts are and when one will reach your stop, worked out by prediction rather than new hardware.

## How this lines up with our timings
- Across our timed rides, the median wait for a cart was 5:56 (5 rides with full records).
- At some stops we saw gaps of 7 to 10 minutes between carts.
- Earlier sessions: median gap 2:36 at Point 1, 1:24 at Point 2, 1:44 at Point 3.
