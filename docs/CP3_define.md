# CP3 Define

## What we found
**From our timings:** median wait 5:56 (range 4:47 to 9:50), median time in the cart 6:44, so waiting is almost as long as the ride. Long gaps: 10:49 at Education Park, 7:41 at Pharmacy Park (4:03 pm), 5:08 at Education Park (other direction), 4:15 at Lovely Bakery. Carts often come in a bunch, then nothing for a while. Lovely Bakery had frequent carts both ways; Education Park had only 5 carts in about 25 minutes in one direction.

**From the interviews:** carts wait to fill; too many students for too few carts; no way to see where carts are; hardware is hard to add.

## CP3.1 Point of view
A student without a vehicle needs to know when the next cart will reach their stop, because carts only leave when full and students can't see where they are.

## CP3.3 Problem statement
Students without vehicles lose a lot of time waiting for campus carts. The carts only leave when full, there are far more students than carts, and nobody can tell when the next one will come. Whatever we come up with has to work without adding new hardware to the carts.

## CP3.4 Main How Might We
How might we tell students when a cart will reach their stop?

## CP3.5 Other HMWs considered
1. How might we cut down the time students spend standing at the stop?
2. How might we make waiting times predictable?
3. How might we help carts fill up without leaving students stuck?
4. How might we help students decide quickly whether to wait or walk?

We chose the main one because not knowing was the biggest complaint and it fits the no-hardware limit.

## CP3.6 Persona
Riya (made-up name), a hostel student without a vehicle. Uses carts between hostel, classes and food. Wants to reach class on time without standing around. Frustrated that carts wait to fill, are too few, and can't be tracked. Anti-persona: a student with their own vehicle.

## CP3.7 Journey map
1. Leaves hostel, no idea when a cart will come (unsure)
2. Walks to the stop, hopes a cart is there (hopeful)
3. Waits, median 5:56, sometimes over 10 min (frustrated, worst point)
4. Boards if there is space, otherwise waits again (anxious)
5. Rides about 6:44 (relieved)
6. Arrives, sometimes late (stressed)

## CP3.8 Affinity clusters
A. Carts wait until full. B. Too many students, too few carts. C. No way to know where or when the cart is. D. Hard to add new hardware. E. Long, uneven gaps and bunching (from timings).
