# CP4 Ideate

Question: How might we tell students when a cart will reach their stop?

## Starter list (32 ideas; reviewed, changed and extended by the team; see AI-use note in the README)
**Knowing when the cart is coming:** 1 app with "next cart in about N min"; 2 predictions from past timings by stop and time of day; 3 driver's phone shares location; 4 students tap "cart just passed"; 5 QR code at each stop; 6 board at busy stops; 7 printed "usual wait" chart; 8 WhatsApp/Telegram bot; 9 alert when a cart leaves the stop before yours; 10 colour sign at stops.

**Cutting the wait:** 11 leave after a set time even if not full; 12 more carts when classes change; 13 express cart between busiest stops; 14 space out departures; 15 hold a cart if another is right behind; 16 send idle carts to the longest queue; 17 peak and quiet schedules; 18 extra carts on long-gap routes.

**Handling the crowd:** 19 "I'm waiting" button; 20 queue lines; 21 students going the same way leave together; 22 book a seat ahead; 23 survey travel times.

**Making waiting less painful:** 24 shade and seats; 25 walking-time map; 26 "just walk" sign; 27 charging point or notice board.

**Wild ones:** 28 one central hub only; 29 carts never stop; 30 students drive; 31 make the wait longer on purpose; 32 swap carts for bike-share.

## Scoring criteria (weights to be agreed by the team and dated before scoring)
Helps students wait less 30, works without new hardware 25, easy to prototype 20, low cost and effort 15, would be accepted 10. Total out of 500.

## Picked
The prediction app (ideas 1 and 2): "next cart in about N minutes" from the timings we already collect, nothing new on the carts.

## Dropped
Idea 11 (leave after a set time). It would likely cut waiting most, but drivers and the transport office decide departures and we could not talk to them.
